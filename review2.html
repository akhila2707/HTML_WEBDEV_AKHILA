Dude Movie Review

Movie Name: Dude (2025)

Genre: Romantic Comedy, Drama

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ (4.2/5)

Dude is a youthful romantic comedy-drama starring Pradeep Ranganathan and Mamitha Baiju. The movie is entertaining with a mix of romance, comedy, family emotions, and a meaningful message.

Pradeep Ranganathan gives a natural and funny performance, while Mamitha Baiju plays her role beautifully. Their chemistry is one of the highlights of the movie.

The first half is fast-paced and filled with comedy and romantic moments. The second half becomes more emotional and focuses on family relationships.

The music and background score add more emotion and energy to the film. The songs are pleasant and suit the story well.

Overall, Dude is a feel-good movie that is enjoyable for family and young audiences. It is a good choice if you like romance, comedy, and emotional stories.
