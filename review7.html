Dear Comrade Movie Review

Movie Name: Dear Comrade (2019)

Genre: Romance, Drama, Sports

Language: Telugu

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.6/5)

Dear Comrade is an emotional Telugu romantic drama starring Vijay Deverakonda and Rashmika Mandanna. Directed by Bharat Kamma, the movie beautifully combines romance, friendship, personal struggles, and an inspiring sports journey.

The story follows Bobby, a passionate and fearless young man who strongly believes in fighting for justice. He falls in love with Lilly, a talented state-level cricketer who dreams of representing India. As they face different challenges in life, their relationship is tested by misunderstandings, personal goals, and emotional struggles.

Vijay Deverakonda delivers a powerful performance with intense emotions and natural acting. Rashmika Mandanna gives one of her best performances as Lilly, portraying the character with confidence, strength, and sensitivity.

The chemistry between Bobby and Lilly is one of the biggest highlights of the movie. Their romantic moments are sweet and emotional, while their conflicts feel realistic and meaningful. The film also talks about self-respect, courage, and standing up against injustice.

The music composed by Justin Prabhakaran is outstanding. Songs like "Kadalalle" and "Nee Neeli Kannullona" became very popular and add more emotion to the story. The background score perfectly matches both the romantic and emotional scenes.

The cinematography is beautiful, with colorful visuals and well-shot sports sequences. The screenplay is emotional and engaging, especially in the second half, where the story becomes more powerful and inspiring.

Overall, Dear Comrade is a heartfelt movie with a strong love story, excellent performances, beautiful music, and an inspiring message about love, dreams, and self-respect. It is a must-watch for fans of emotional romantic dramas.
