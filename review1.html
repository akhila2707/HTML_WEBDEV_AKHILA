Vishwanathan & Sons Movie Review

If you're looking for a feel-good family movie with romance, comedy, and emotions, Vishwanathan & Sons is a good pick. It's directed by Venky Atluri and stars Suriya, Mamitha Baiju, Radhika Sarathkumar, and Nassar. The film released in August 2026 and has received mostly positive reviews from critics and audiences. What is good? ❤️

Suriya is the biggest strength of the movie. He gives a charming and emotional performance without relying on mass action scenes. Vishwanath and Sons Movie Rating

Mamitha Baiju surprises with a confident performance and has great chemistry with Suriya.

Radhika Sarathkumar is excellent as the mother and brings both comedy and emotional moments.

The movie has a warm family atmosphere with plenty of funny scenes and heartfelt moments. Vishwanath and Sons Movie Rating

What could have been better? 🤏

The movie is a little long (around 2 hours 40 minutes). Some parts of the second half feel slower and could have been shorter. One relationship in the story has an age-gap, which some viewers found uncomfortable, while others felt the film handled it maturely. The Week

Should you watch it?

Yes, especially if you like: Family entertainers. Emotional love stories. Suriya's acting. Movies like Lucky Baskhar, Hi Nanna, or Sita Ramam with a wholesome emotional vibe. Skip it if you want: Fast-paced action. Thriller or mystery. Heavy mass fights throughout the movie.
