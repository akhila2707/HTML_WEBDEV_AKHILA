Hi Nanna Movie Review

Movie Name: Hi Nanna (2023)

Genre: Romance, Family Drama, Emotional

Language: Telugu

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.8/5)

Hi Nanna is a heartwarming Telugu romantic family drama starring Nani, Mrunal Thakur, and Baby Kiara Khanna. Directed by Shouryuv, the movie beautifully combines love, family emotions, and the special bond between a father and his daughter.

The story follows Viraj, a caring father who raises his young daughter with love and affection. His daughter is curious about her mother and often asks questions about her. As Viraj tells her a story from the past, the movie slowly reveals an emotional journey filled with love, sacrifice, and hope.

Nani delivers a wonderful performance as a loving father, showing both strength and vulnerability. Mrunal Thakur shines with her graceful acting and emotional expressions, while Baby Kiara steals everyone's heart with her innocent and natural performance.

The chemistry between Nani and Mrunal Thakur is beautiful and emotional. Their romantic scenes feel genuine, and the father-daughter relationship is the biggest highlight of the movie. Many scenes are touching and leave a lasting emotional impact.

The music composed by Hesham Abdul Wahab is one of the strongest parts of the film. Songs like "Samayama" and the emotional background score perfectly match the mood of the story and make many scenes even more memorable.

The cinematography is soft, colorful, and visually beautiful. The screenplay moves at a calm pace, allowing the audience to connect deeply with the characters and their emotions.

Overall, Hi Nanna is a beautiful and emotional movie that celebrates love, family, forgiveness, and second chances. It is a perfect feel-good family entertainer and a must-watch for anyone who enjoys heartfelt romantic and emotional stories.
